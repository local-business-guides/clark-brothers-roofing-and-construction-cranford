What to ask about the roofing work

Before agreeing to a project, make sure you understand what the contractor is proposing. If siding, gutters, or windows come up, ask how those items fit into the overall plan rather than treating them as automatic additions.

A short checklist can help you keep the conversation focused:

  • Discuss your requirements and budget before settling on a plan.
