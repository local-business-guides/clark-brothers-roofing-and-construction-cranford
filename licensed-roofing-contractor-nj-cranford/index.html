Before hiring, gather your questions and make sure you understand the proposed scope. You don’t need to diagnose a roof problem yourself. Describe what you’ve noticed, identify the area you’re concerned about, and ask a professional to explain what may need attention.

Use this checklist during the conversation:

  • Get a clear description of the roofing work being proposed.
  • Ask whether siding, gutters, or windows are relevant to your project.
  • Discuss how the crew will take care of your property during the work.
