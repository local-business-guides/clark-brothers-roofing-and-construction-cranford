What to discuss before work begins

The right scope depends on the project. When you speak with a contractor, describe what you've observed rather than guessing at the cause. Ask what the next steps are, what the work would cover, and what decisions you'll need to make. If you have questions about materials, timing, or how the work may affect access to the property, bring them up before scheduling.

Before the conversation, you can prepare by gathering:
- A brief list of concerns and any changes you've noticed.
- Photos you already have, if they help explain the issue.
- Your preferred timing and a budget range you're comfortable discussing.
- Questions about protecting the home and keeping the work area manageable.

Avoid choosing a contractor based only on a quick assumption about what the roof needs. Don't overlook related concerns, but don't agree to extra work without understanding its purpose and scope. If you see a problem you can't assess safely, leave the inspection to a professional.
